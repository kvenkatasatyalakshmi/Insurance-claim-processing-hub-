# Insurance Claim Processing Hub

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Insurance Claim Processing Hub** is a ServiceNow-based application designed to streamline insurance claim management. It helps manage claims, track required documents, verify claim requirements, and support risk assessment throughout the claim processing lifecycle.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Simplify insurance claim tracking and management.
* Maintain claim-related document information.
* Track mandatory and pending claim requirements.
* Automate claim requirement creation based on claim type.
* Support risk identification and claim decision-making.

## ⚙️ Technologies Used

* ServiceNow Platform
* ServiceNow Tables and Forms
* Flow Designer
* Decision Tables
* ServiceNow Update Sets

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Claim Management

Create and manage insurance claims with details such as claim number, claim type, claim amount, incident date, claim status, and risk level.

### 2. Claim Document Management

Maintain claim-related document details, document types, verification statuses, and extracted text.

### 3. Claim Requirement Tracking

Track required documents and other claim requirements, including mandatory status, due dates, and requirement status.

### 4. Automated Claim Processing

Use Flow Designer and a decision table to determine required documents based on the claim type and create corresponding claim requirement records.

### 5. Risk Assessment Support

Maintain risk indicators and risk-related information to support the claim assessment process.

## 🔄 Claim Processing Workflow

1. Create an insurance claim.
2. Submit the claim for processing.
3. Determine the required documents based on the claim type.
4. Create and track the corresponding claim requirements.
5. Verify documents and assess claim risk.
6. Review the claim and update its status through the processing lifecycle.

## 🧰 Application Components

* Claim table
* Claim Document table
* Claim Requirement table
* Risk Indicator table
* Insurance Claim Processing Flow
* Claim Required Documents decision table
